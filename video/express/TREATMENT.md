## 1. Inventory
- Name: rederive, to arrive at a known result by your own derivation. Mark ∂ (supplied).
- Non-obvious behaviour: unlike an explainer it never states the answer. It poses the problem, lets your design break on a real constraint, and names the result only when your revision matches the real code.
- Objects: the problem bubble with its step line, your typed answer, the design drawn live (a browser, a server with route rows, locks, a gate, a chain of boxes, the request dot), the constraint, the named result.
- The decision on screen: where Express puts the login check. Per handler (forty routes, one missed), one function before any route (then three more jobs want that spot), a list where each function calls the next: the middleware chain.
- Pain: you built it with AI, or you are learning a repo, and cannot explain why it is built that way.
- Category clichés banned: terminal, code rain, neural graphic, sparkles, a progress bar.

## 2. Concepts
- (a) Verb made literal: the design is drawn as you type it, and the constraint physically breaks the drawing (a request slips into an unguarded route). Owned: only rederive makes you draw the design before it names it. **Chosen.**
- (b) Pain made visible: the interview blank. Dropped by Eric (no interview hook).
- (c) Formal: one canvas, one continuous take, the chat lives above and below the drawing, the ∂ avatar ends as the mark via an iris. **Grafted** into (a).

## 3. Concept
- Logline: you design where the login check lives, it breaks twice, and Express's middleware chain is what you end up drawing. (14 words)
- Device: the request, one ink dot. It waits at the server's edge (bar 4), passes a lock (bar 6), slips into the unguarded route (bar 7), passes the gate (bar 10), bounces without a token (bar 11), steps through the chain and lands green (bar 16). Its last position seeds the iris that becomes the ∂ lockup.
- Accent #1A7F37 means "resolved: your design matches the real one". It appears when the request lands through the chain (bar 16), on "middleware chain" in the payoff line, and as the iris seed; nowhere on the end card except the mark's seed.
- Stage: white paper, ink #0D1117 (brand), Geist 500/650, Geist Mono for routes and the counter.
- Arc: hook (the proposition, then the command names Express) → problem 1 → the lock per handler breaks on the fortieth route → the gate → the gate gets crowded → the chain → the name → lockup.

## 6. Tests
- Deletion: without the drawing beats the exchange still reads as a Socratic dialogue; without the dialogue the drawing is a diagram of middleware with no reason to exist. Both halves carry.
- Specificity: a simpler tool would explain middleware with a diagram. This film never explains it; the user draws three designs and the third is the real one. The forty-row scroll with one missed lock is the part that is hard, and it is shown.
- Feature word: muted, a stranger sees a chat where the person types and a drawing beneath changes with each answer, then a word gets named. They would say "it made me design it".
- Mark: brand supplied (∂ from assets/logo.png), no exploration needed.
